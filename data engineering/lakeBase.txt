->Serverless postgreSql differ from traditional postgresql because it decouples compute(CPU/RAM) from storage allowing to scale and shut off when not in use.
	-Shared/Distributed storage.
	-Compute nodes are temporary and stateless.
	==Compute pauses completely during inactivity.
	==Pay for active compute seconds and storage.
	-Offers git-style database branching. Let's us to use large data(>1TB) in seconds in different env.
		-Storage costs scale only when modifications are made.
	-AWS aurora and Google AlloyDB separates compute and storage only these DB engines can access data.
-Databricks wants to build OLTP DB that:
    -Has compute and storage separate.
    -Stores data in an open format to enable interoperability.
    -Has the ability to branch out.
-Lakebase resizes compute continuously based on live workload.
